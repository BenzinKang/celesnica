History

[B]History

Two Aliases

Celesnica

Early 2026 — now

Before early 2026

No-One CN

Before launching Celesnica in early 2026, he released music under the alias No-One CN, focusing mainly on commercial EDM and collaborative projects. During this period, he was a member of Tencent Tengfei Alliance and served as the head of Trailblazers Radar. He was shortlisted for Music China 2024, placed 2nd in China and 14th globally in the SKIO Music x Nurko “Retraced” Remix Contest, and contributed music to the Magnolia International Music Festival in China. His releases appeared on labels including SN, 2088 RECORDS, Stardust Records, Quadrant Shield, Vultures Records, Taisheng Music, and Diary Music, with his Stardust Records releases accumulating over 100K Spotify streams.

Early 2026 — now

Celesnica

As Celesnica, he has expanded into a wider range of electronic styles and developed a more personal artistic direction. He actively builds his presence across Instagram, Spotify, and SoundCloud, and has contributed music to Yostar's Stella Sora. He is a member of Guangzhou Zhacai Music Studio, a manager and website developer at 2088 RECORDS, and a team member and artist at VISION SOUND. He also won Vesk Green's “未眠 (No Sleep)” Remix Contest, earning an official release for his remix.
